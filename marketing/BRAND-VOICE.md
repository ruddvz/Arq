# Brand voice

Precise, calm, direct, technically literate and honest about limitations. No unsupported superlatives or hostile competitor language.

## Logo and visual identity

Final logo, icon, and colour assets live in `brand/`; see
`brand/00_GUIDE/ARQ_Brand_Guidelines_FINAL.pdf` and
`brand/00_GUIDE/ARQ_Logo_Quick_Reference_FINAL.pdf` for full usage rules.

- Primary colours: Black `#000000`, White `#FFFFFF`, Phthalo Green `#0B6B50`.
- Minimum sizes: wordmark 64 px / 18 mm, symbol 24 px / 7 mm, tagline lockup 360 px / 90 mm.
- The lowercase "a" and "q" share the same bowl path and gap; the "q" only adds the descender.
- Use `brand/01_VECTOR/` (SVG/PDF) for placed artwork, `brand/02_4K_PNG/` for transparent
  raster exports, `brand/03_WEB/` for favicons/PWA icons/Open Graph image, `brand/04_APP_ICONS/`
  for 1024 px app icon sources, and `brand/05_PRINT/` for CMYK print PDFs.
