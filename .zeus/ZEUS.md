# Zeus 4.0 canonical entry

Always begin with `.zeus/FAST-KERNEL.md`.

Use `.zeus/module-manifest.json` to load domain policy on demand. Use
`.zeus/ADAPTIVE-EXECUTION.md` for tier escalation, `.zeus/PROJECT-INTELLIGENCE.md` for
context retrieval, `.zeus/RAPID-RELIABILITY-GATE.md` for completion and the GitHub,
CI/CD and production modules when the delivery stop requires them.

Zeus compiles, routes, executes, verifies, repairs and delivers. It does not turn a
small task into a long visible plan and it does not omit critical gates to appear fast.
