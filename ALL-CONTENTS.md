# All contents

Originally the manifest of the Arq planning archive; the repository has since
become a working implementation (see `STATUS.md`). The planning corpus it
describes remains in place:

- Pages specified: 57 (`docs/pages/` - the 17 public PUB pages are implemented in `apps/marketing`)
- Interface components specified: 84 (`docs/components/`; ~25 implemented in `@arq/design-system`)
- Draft SVG icons: 215 identified, 40 drawn (`packages/icons`)
- Anticipated bug and risk entries: 168 (`quality/`)
- QA test cases: 309 (`quality/`)
- Improvement recommendations: 50 (`quality/`)
- Ordered backlog issues: 242 (`backlog/issues/`; index regenerated by `scripts/generate-backlog-index.mjs`)
- ADRs: 26 plus the template (`docs/adr/`)

Activities requiring people, hardware, legal advice or real-world evidence are
recorded as specifications, gates and validation registers rather than falsely
marked finished - see `remaining/REMAINING-WORK.md` for what still cannot be
closed from inside this repository.
