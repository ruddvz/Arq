# Scope and conformance

This candidate specification defines package-only AOGRP behaviour. Normative terms MUST, MUST NOT, SHOULD, and MAY indicate intended conformance requirements. No production identifier is allocated.

Conformance classes: Preflight Reader, Preserving Reader, Full Reader, Portable Writer, Append Writer, Materialisation Adapter, Sync Peer, Domain Pack, MCP Host, and Conformance Suite. A product may claim only classes proven by named tests and fixtures.
