# Roadmap and capability ladder

## Stage 0: repository truth reconciliation

Resolve repository, immutable HEAD, worktree, open PRs, accepted ADRs, current `.arq` identity, schemas, migrations, file-opening code, tests, and delivery stop. Read `CLAUDE.md`, `.zeus/FAST-KERNEL.md`, and repository invariants.

## Stage 1: trustworthy portable publication

- Bounded read-only preflight.
- Structured compatibility verdict.
- Copy-on-write candidate publication.
- Fresh-reader integrity and semantic comparison.
- Source-preserving failure behaviour.
- Healthy and damaged golden fixtures.

## Stage 2: canonical operations and revisions

- Registered exact quantities.
- Deterministic operation encoding.
- Atomic groups and immutable revisions.
- Grouped undo and provenance.
- Snapshot and replay evidence.

## Stage 3: architecture protected workflow

Complete the repository-governed residential plan, coordinated 3D, sheets, dimensions, PDF export, save, reopen, recovery, and modification workflow.

## Stage 4: kernel-neutral precision feature slice

Implement constrained sketch, extrusion, boolean, fillet or chamfer subset, topology selection, validation, and exact dimensional change using a kernel bakeoff winner.

## Stage 5: small assembly demonstrator

Create an adjustable desk or mechanical fixture with parts, joints, clearances, configurations, drawings, save/reopen, AI proposal, and grouped undo.

## Stage 6: interchange profiles

Add one open BIM profile and one product geometry profile with object-level fidelity evidence. Proprietary formats follow only after SDK and licensing evaluation.

## Stage 7: optional collaboration

Immutable revision push/pull, pinned references, branches, explicit merge, access control, and disaster recovery. Do not begin with real-time geometry co-editing.

## Stage 8: vehicle and industrial design pack

Packaging, surfaces, assemblies, configurations, analysis adapters, manufacturing evidence, and controlled optimisation.

## Stage 9: aerospace concept pack

Parametric airframe concepts, mass properties, aerodynamic and structural adapters, system interfaces, and explicit feasibility limitations. No certification claims.

## Stage 10: city federation

Terrain, geospatial frames, districts, networks, buildings, infrastructure, partial loading, LOD, simulations, and federated release baselines.
