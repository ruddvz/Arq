# Plugin and domain-pack sandbox

Domain packs register schemas and operations through signed manifests and explicit capabilities. They do not receive raw SQLite handles. Execution occurs in an isolated worker or process with CPU, memory, recursion, asset, network, and time limits. A pack cannot enlarge its own permissions, replace core validators, suppress diagnostics, or change accepted project state outside typed operations.

## Mandatory evidence

A production claim requires repository tests, revision-bound artefacts, and failure-path verification. Package-local demonstrations are informative only.
