# Competitive position

ARQ should not claim superiority because its container is custom. Its defensible position is evidence-rich precision authoring:

- inspectable semantic intent;
- exact operation provenance;
- portable revision identity;
- truthful fidelity reporting;
- local recovery;
- reviewable automation;
- domain-pack extensibility;
- partial loading and federation;
- replaceable kernels and storage engines.

Each advantage requires released evidence. Until then it remains a target.
