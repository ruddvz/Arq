# Participant screener

Collect:

- role;
- years in practice;
- practice size;
- main project types;
- software used;
- operating systems;
- iPad use;
- recent small residential or renovation project;
- willingness to screen share a non-confidential workflow;
- location and time zone;
- permission for notes or recording.

Target mix:

- independent architects;
- practices with 2 to 10 people;
- renovation-focused architects;
- students;
- BIM coordinators;
- interior architects.
