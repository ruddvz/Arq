# Community research protocol

Sources may include Reddit, professional forums, vendor forums, GitHub issues,
Capterra and G2.

Rules:

- Treat posts as qualitative evidence.
- Record date, product version and context.
- Separate widespread themes from one-off defects.
- Do not convert comments into invented statistics.
- Preserve contradictory views.
- Confirm technical claims with primary documentation.
- Avoid copying long copyrighted passages.
- Redact personal and project information.
- Recheck current product behaviour before making a product decision.
