# Arq brand voice

## Persona

Arq sounds like a technically competent architect-engineer explaining a professional tool to another professional.

Not stiff. Not cute. Not salesy.

## Traits

**Precise**  
Names real objects, units, states, and consequences.

**Calm**  
No panic language, no cheerleading, no decorative urgency.

**Direct**  
Leads with the action or fact.

**Inspectable**  
Explains why, source, limits, and affected scope when they matter.

**Restrained**  
Personality comes from confidence and specificity, not slogans every three lines.

## Public marketing range

Arq may use a memorable headline.

It should not use a memorable headline to smuggle in a stronger claim than the body can support.

Good:

- Draw plans that behave like buildings.
- One model, several views.
- Format support, stated exactly.

Use carefully:

- Architecture made simple.

Avoid:

- The future of architecture.
- The only CAD you will ever need.
- Revit, finally fixed.
- Design anything, anywhere.

## Competitive posture

Arq can say:

> Arq is not intended to replace every AutoCAD, Revit, Archicad, SketchUp, Rhino, or Blender workflow.

Arq should not say:

> CAD marketing lies.

The first statement explains scope. The second asks the user to trust attitude instead of evidence.

## Voice by state

**Normal**
Quiet and concise.

**Warning**
Specific, not alarming.

**Blocking error**
Firm about the block, clear about unchanged state.

**Destructive action**
Explicit about dependencies and irreversible effects.

**Recovery**
Reassuring only through facts.

**Security**
Evidence-led. No badge language without a real badge.

**AI**
Neutral, reviewable, provenance-aware.

## Tagline

Current repository tagline: **Architecture made simple.**

Treat the tagline as brand copy, not a functional claim. Do not use it to imply that complex professional requirements are automatically handled.
