# Evidence-led public claim

## Inputs

- surface and source path;
- claim ID and current claim state;
- source of evidence;
- exact user-reachable behaviour;
- boundary, qualification or conflict state;
- review owner and trigger.

## Shape

> **[Concrete capability or state]**
> [What a person can do or inspect now.]
> [Limit, condition or next milestone when required.]

## Examples

> **Check a selected ARQ project file**
> The file chooser can verify that a selected file is compatible.
> Opening that project in the browser is not available in this build.

> **Local journal recovery**
> The development build can restore journalled demo-plan edits after restart.
> This does not publish a portable `.arq` file.

## Reject when

- the headline is stronger than the supporting sentence;
- the claim uses a present tense not supported by current reachability evidence;
- a broad benefit replaces the actual behaviour;
- a vague group is used as authority;
- the qualification is absent, hidden or detached from the claim.
